# web/src/sim — live Kelvin–Helmholtz hero (WebGL2, no dependency)

- **Equations**: 2D incompressible Navier–Stokes, ∂ₜ**u** + (**u**·∇)**u** = −∇p + ν∇²**u**, ∇·**u** = 0, on [0, Lx] × [0, 1], periodic in x, free-slip walls in y; two passive dyes (smoke, fluorescein) ∂ₜc + **u**·∇c = 0. Base flow u = tanh((y − ½)/δ), δ = 1/28 (fastest KH wavelength ≈ 14δ = ½), ΔU = 2, Re = Uδ/ν = 1000 (nominal; the semi-Lagrangian interpolation adds numerical viscosity), seeded with the fundamental (Lx/λ billows) + a weak random-phase subharmonic (→ pairing).
- **Scheme** (stable fluids, colocated grid, one step per 1/60 s, dt = 0.002): semi-Lagrangian advection of **u** + explicit viscosity (1 pass), divergence (1), 24 warm-started Jacobi sweeps (24), gradient subtraction (1); MacCormack/BFECC advection of the dyes with a min–max limiter (2 passes) on a 3× finer grid; display (1). About 30 passes per frame. MacCormack on the velocity was tried and dropped: it feeds the colocated odd–even pressure mode (dark crosses in the cores).
- **Grids**: 512×256 flow / 1536×768 dyes on desktop (Lx = 2…4 from the canvas aspect so the view never repeats); 256×128 / 512×256 on phones or ≤ 4 cores; DPR ≤ 1.5 (1 on phones).
- **Look**: silver smoke #EDEBE6 on lab black #0B0B0B, one fluorescein filament #C6F03C just above the layer, animated grain, vignette; the view drifts downstream. Every 32 s the layer is re-seeded and the old dyes cross-fade out (4 s).
- **Opening**: the prerendered poster is the state the solver starts from (the tanh layer as parallel smoke lines at the solver's spacing and brightness envelope, one straight fluorescein filament 1.5δ above the layer: level sets of Stuart's ψ = ln(cosh y − ρ cos x) at ρ = 0.05; the prerendered SVG draws its open lines with 16 points per period, ≈ 4× lighter than at 64, the closed eyes at full sampling), so the cross-fade to the live canvas is continuous and the reader watches the layer roll up. Without a solver (WebGL2 refused), the poster advances through the exact Stuart family, ρ = 0.05 → 0.55 over 4.2 s: every frame is an exact steady Euler solution (a family of solutions, not a time evolution). Reduced motion: ρ = 0.55 at once.
- **Reader input**: press and drag on the canvas lays fluorescein along the pointer path (`SPLAT_DYE`: Gaussian tube of radius ≈ 2 dye texels, smoke displaced, clamped to 1) plus a gentle momentum impulse (`SPLAT`) only while pressed; holding still is a dye port. `touch-action: pan-y`: vertical swipes scroll the page. No forcing without a press.
- **Controls** (top-right): Pause/Play and a one-line hint; no numeric readout (`sim.stats()` is kept for debugging, on the main-thread path only).
- **Testing**: `?sim=soft` in the URL accepts a software GL renderer (headless SwiftShader screenshots: `node web/scripts/shoot.mjs <dir> "/?sim=soft"`).
- **Start-up**: the solver is created after `load` + `requestIdleCallback` (fallback 1.2 s); the SVG poster shows until the first frame. Where the browser allows it the solver runs in a worker on an OffscreenCanvas (`host.ts`, `kh.worker.ts`): the worker first probes a 1×1 WebGL2 context of its own, and only a hardware renderer receives the canvas (`transferControlToOffscreen` cannot be undone); no worker, no WebGL2 in workers, `file://` or a blocked worker → the solver on the main thread, created in short tasks (shader programs issued together and polled with `KHR_parallel_shader_compile`, else checked one per task). A software GL renderer (SwiftShader, llvmpipe, Basic Render…) is refused, already at context creation (`failIfMajorPerformanceCaveat`): poster only. Before (2026-10-02) the home page's total blocking time under Lighthouse was ≈ 1 s: the software context was created and inspected on the main thread, and the poster family recomputed ≈ 15 000 points per frame at 60 Hz. The poster family now updates at most 30 times a second with a number of points per line adapted to the measured cost of a frame (8–64 per period, under ~6 ms), and the final frame is redrawn at full resolution a few lines per task.
- **Degradation**: 32F flow if `EXT_color_buffer_float` + `OES_texture_float_linear`, else half floats, else static SVG poster (Stuart cat's-eye streamlines); < 40 fps over 2 s → grid halved (twice at most); paused off-screen / hidden tab; `prefers-reduced-motion` → 480 steps off-screen in time slices (~25 ms per task, `sim.still`), one still frame.
- **Cost** (headless Chrome/ANGLE-Vulkan on an L40S, canvas 1280×560): 0.12 ms per frame (1 step + render, GPU-synced) at 512×256/×3, 60 fps rAF-bound in the component; no integrated-GPU measurement yet (pass count: ≈ 30 passes on 0.13 M cells + 2 on 1.2 M dye texels).
- **Files**: `kh.ts` (engine, `createKHSim(canvas | OffscreenCanvas, opts)`, async), `kh.worker.ts` (the engine in a worker), `host.ts` (worker or main thread), `shaders.ts` (GLSL ES 3.00), `FluidHero.tsx` (React wrapper, poster, Pause toggle, pointer forwarding). ≈ 21 KB minified, 7.7 KB gzip.
