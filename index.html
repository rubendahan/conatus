    Write

    The equation on the page is the equation that runs

    Conatus reads the problem as mathematics, compiles it for the CPU or the GPU, and tells you exactly what it did. Nothing changes behind your back. Below, the first tutorial as it ran, and what it printed.

    1. Declare the domain, the fields and the equations, as written on paper.
    2. Discretise and check. A rank report refuses a missing or extra boundary condition before anything runs.
    3. Solve, and see how far to trust it. Every result comes with its own checks.

    What it does

    From the equations to the structure of the flow

    1. Write

      Equations as on paper

      Declare the domain, the fields and the equations in their usual form; name the boundaries and say what holds there. Conatus places the rest and tells you what it did.

    2. Simulate

      On a CPU or a GPU

      The same model runs on your laptop or on a GPU with one keyword. Resolution, scheme and time step are always yours to choose.

    3. Analyse

      Where a flow becomes unstable

      Eigenvalues with their residuals, critical parameters, exact adjoints, transient growth and Floquet analysis of time-periodic flows.

    4. Discover

      Orbits and bifurcations

      Equilibria, travelling waves and periodic orbits by Newton–Krylov; branches followed through folds, pitchforks and Hopf points.

    5. Keep track

      Every run, recorded

      Parameters, code version and results are kept for each run, so you can find it, compare it and reproduce it later.
