add_bc(*conditions, into=None)[source]¶

Add boundary conditions and their tau terms (standard tau), declared in explain().

Each condition is an expression meaning expr = 0 or a tuple (lhs, rhs). All conditions of one call act along one coordinate (read off their interp nodes). For each condition one tau variable (constant along that coordinate) is created and lifted into the next highest mode of the target equation’s basis: N-1, N-2, … in the order of the conditions. The target is the only bulk equation along the coordinate, or into (equation index) when there are several: never guessed. With a symmetry subspace the taus and their parities must be declared by hand (refused here).

Parameters:

  • conditions (Any)

  • into (int | None)
