Expert layer by module¶

Every public object re-exported by conatus.expert, grouped by the module that defines it and documented from its docstring.

conatus.verify¶

Verification tools: MMS, convergence studies, reports — never a CORRECT flag.

Check(name, value, threshold, what)

VerificationReport(checks)

verify(pevp, mode[, critical, rel_h])

Residuals, rank, reconstruction, spectral tail, sensitivity Taylor test, critical point.

conatus.ir¶

Mathematical IR: coordinates, bases, domains, fields, expressions, problems.

CartesianCoordinates(*names)

Cartesian coordinates in 1, 2 or 3 dimensions (identity metric).

PolarCoordinates(azimuth, radius)

(phi, r) on the disk or annulus.

CylindricalCoordinates(azimuth, radius, axial)

(phi, r, z) for pipes and periodic cylinders.

S2Coordinates(azimuth, colatitude)

(phi, theta) on the sphere of radius R.

SphericalCoordinates(azimuth, colatitude, radius)

(phi, theta, r) for the ball and the spherical shell.

RealFourier(coord, size, bounds[, dealias, grid])

Real Fourier series (cos/sin pairs) for real-valued data.

ComplexFourier(coord, size, bounds[, ...])

Complex Fourier series exp(i k x).

Jacobi(coord, size, bounds, a, b[, dealias, ...])

Jacobi polynomials P_n^(a,b) on a bounded interval (mapped from [-1, 1]).

Chebyshev(coord, size, bounds[, dealias, grid])

Chebyshev T_n = Jacobi(-1/2, -1/2) up to normalisation.

ChebyshevT

ChebyshevU(coord, size, bounds[, dealias])

Chebyshev polynomials of the second kind U_n = C_n^(1) (Dedalus ChebyshevU).

ChebyshevV(coord, size, bounds[, dealias])

Gegenbauer C_n^(2), under Dedalus's name ChebyshevV (= Ultraspherical(alpha=2)).

Legendre(coord, size, bounds[, dealias])

Legendre P_n = Jacobi(0, 0).

Ultraspherical(coord, size, bounds, lam[, ...])

Ultraspherical (Gegenbauer) C_n^(lambda) = Jacobi(lambda-1/2, lambda-1/2) up to norm.

DiskBasis(coord, size[, radius, dealias])

Radial basis of the full disk r in [0, R], regular at the axis (Vasil et al., JCP 2016).

AnnulusBasis(coord, size, radii[, dealias])

Radial basis of the annulus r in [ri, ro] (0 < ri): Chebyshev T_n(t), r = c + h t.

SphereBasis(coord, size[, radius, dealias])

Colatitude part of the spin-weighted spherical harmonics (Vasil et al. 2019), attached to theta of S2Coordinates or SphericalCoordinates, next to a Fourier basis in phi.

ShellBasis(coord, size, radii[, dealias])

Spherical shell ri <= r <= ro (0 < ri): Chebyshev T_n(t), r = c + h t, for every spin component; equations in a declared r-multiplied form (each row times the smallest power of r that makes its coefficients polynomial, as Dedalus's r^-k Jacobi(k-1/2, k-1/2) derivative bases).

BallBasis(coord, size[, radius, dealias])

Full ball 0 <= r <= R, regular at the centre (Vasil et al. 2019, Lecoanet et al. 2019): the regularity component sigma of degree l is rho^k Q_n^(a, k + 1/2)(2 rho^2 - 1), k = l + sigma, n < size - floor(l/2) (Dedalus's BallBasis truncation), a = derivative level (0 for fields).

Hermite(coord, size[, center, scale, dealias])

Hermite functions on the whole line: f(x) = sum_n c_n psi_n((x - center)/scale), with psi_n(y) = H_n(y) exp(-y^2/2) / sqrt(2^n n! sqrt(pi)) (orthonormal).

Laguerre(coord, size[, start, scale, dealias])

Laguerre functions on the half line x >= start: f(x) = sum_n c_n L_n(y) exp(-y/2), y = (x - start)/scale (orthonormal).

CompoundChebyshev(coord, sizes, breakpoints)

Chebyshev T series on consecutive subintervals [x_0, x_1], [x_1, x_2], ..., each with its own size (sizes); coefficients stored piece after piece.

Domain(coordsys, bases)

Tensor-product domain: one coordinate system, bases for a subset of its coords.

Field(name, domain[, tensorsig, dtype, symmetry])

A named unknown / coefficient field on a domain.

VectorField(name, domain[, dtype, symmetry])

Vector field: a Field with one index in the domain's coordinate system.

TensorField(name, domain, rank[, dtype, ...])

Tensor field of the given rank on the domain's coordinate system.

Parameter(name[, value, dtype])

Named scalar parameter (Re, Ra, eigenvalue...).

dot(a, b)

Contraction of the last index of a with the first index of b (rank >= 1).

cross(a, b)

a x b for two vectors of the same 3D coordinate system.

outer(a, b)

Outer (tensor) product; a plain product when one factor is a scalar.

conj(x)

Complex conjugate (the identity on real expressions).

comp(x, index)

Component index of a tensor along its first index.

diff(x, coord)

Partial derivative of x along coord (zero if x does not depend on it).

grad(x[, coordsys])

Gradient of x (adds a first index) in coordsys, by default that of x's domain.

div(x)

Divergence: contracts the derivative with the first index of x (rank >= 1).

lap(x[, coordsys])

Laplacian of x in coordsys, by default that of x's domain.

curl(x[, coordsys])

Curl: of a vector in 3D (a vector), of a vector in 2D (a scalar), of a scalar in 2D (a vector).

dt(x)

Time derivative (IVP left-hand side, or sigma in a linearized EVP).

integ(x[, coords])

Integral of x over coords (all coordinates of its domain by default).

interp(x, coord, position[, side])

Value of x at coord = position (a number, 'left' or 'right'): traces and BCs.

lift(x, basis, n[, lam, piece])

Tau term: x times the polynomial of index N+n of basis (lam=0) or of the ultraspherical C^(lam) basis of the same size and interval (lam >= 1, Chebyshev only): lifting an order-q equation in C^(q) puts its taus in the last q rows of the ultraspherical equation, the classic Olver–Townsend tau (no spurious eigenvalues for dt lap^2-type operators, which T-lifts produce).

unit_vector(coord)

Constant unit vector e_coord of a Cartesian (or the z of a cylindrical) system.

exp(x)

Pointwise exponential (non-polynomial: evaluated on the grid, aliasing reported).

log(x)

Pointwise natural logarithm (non-polynomial: evaluated on the grid, aliasing reported).

sin(x)

Pointwise sine (non-polynomial: evaluated on the grid, aliasing reported).

cos(x)

Pointwise cosine (non-polynomial: evaluated on the grid, aliasing reported).

tanh(x)

Pointwise hyperbolic tangent (non-polynomial: evaluated on the grid, aliasing reported).

sqrt(x)

Pointwise square root (non-polynomial: evaluated on the grid, aliasing reported).

absolute(x)

Pointwise absolute value (non-polynomial: evaluated on the grid, aliasing reported).

sign(x)

Pointwise sign (non-polynomial: evaluated on the grid, aliasing reported).

Problem(variables, *[, name, symmetry, ...])

Common machinery: variables, equations, LHS/RHS checks, counting, explain().

IVP(variables, *[, name, symmetry, namespace])

Initial value problem: M dt(X) + L X = F(X, t).

LBVP(variables, *[, name, symmetry, namespace])

Linear boundary value problem: L X = F, F independent of X.

EVP(variables, eigenvalue, *[, name, ...])

Generalized eigenvalue problem L X = -sigma M X written with sigma in the LHS; RHS = 0.

NLBVP(variables, *[, name, symmetry, namespace])

Nonlinear boundary value problem F(X) = 0, F = LHS - RHS; any term may be nonlinear.

NonlinearLHSError([message, where, why, ...])

The LHS contains a term that is not linear in the problem variables.

ProblemError

RankReport(variable_components, ...[, ...])

IR-level count of a problem: unknown and equation components, gauges and one DirectionReport per non-periodic coordinate; status and explain().

EVEN

+1 (even / symmetric) or -1 (odd / antisymmetric).

ODD

+1 (even / symmetric) or -1 (odd / antisymmetric).

Reflection(coord[, about])

Reflection x_c -> 2*about - x_c.

SymmetrySubspace(*generators, **parities)

Invariant subspace of a group generated by reflections, with field parities.

SymmetryError([message, where, why, fix, doc])

Inconsistent symmetry declaration.

SymmetryBreakingError([message, where, why, ...])

A term does not have the parity required by the declared subspace.

ShiftReflect(coord, shift)

Shift-and-reflect of a periodic pipe or cylinder: phi -> -phi composed with the half-period shift z -> z + Lz/2 of the axis,

AzimuthalMFold(coord[, m0])

Rotation phi -> phi + 2 pi / m0 about the axis (disk, annulus, cylinder, sphere, shell, ball).

EquatorialParity(coordsys)

Equatorial reflection z -> -z (theta -> pi - theta) of S2 or spherical coordinates: the parity (-1)^(l+m) of Y_l^m (spin components: u_+ <-> -u_-).

MappedChebyshev(coord, size, map[, dealias, ...])

Chebyshev T_n(xi) with a declared map y = m(xi) onto an (semi-)infinite interval.

RationalMap(L[, origin, contour])

Half line y in [origin, inf): y = origin + L (1 + xi)/(1 - xi) (rational Chebyshev TL).

TanhMap(L[, origin, contour])

Infinite line y in (-inf, inf): y = origin + L artanh(xi).

TanMap(L[, origin, contour])

Infinite line y in (-inf, inf): y = origin + L tan(pi xi / 2).

parse_equation(text[, namespace])

Parse "lhs = rhs" into an Equation (no eval).

parse_expr(text[, namespace])

Parse one expression (no '=') into an IR expression (a number stays a Constant).

conatus.flows¶

Usual base flows and their domains, stated explicitly (no hidden defaults).

ChannelBaseFlow(y, basis, domain, U, ncc, ...)

Wall-normal coordinate y, its Chebyshev basis and domain, base flow U and NCC data.

channel(N, base, *[, interval, dealias])

Plane channel in y with base flow base ('poiseuille', 'couette', or None).

conatus.units¶

Units and nondimensionalizations.

UnitSystem(name, family[, scales, reynolds])

A nondimensionalization: named reference scales within a family, and its Reynolds group.

convert(value, kind, frm, to)

Express value (a kind quantity written in system frm) in system to.

Energy(value, convention)

An energy value tagged with its convention; conversion only by an explicit call.

ChannelUnits(Re, based_on)

A channel Reynolds number with its convention stated (based_on is mandatory).

conatus.discretize¶

Spectral discretization: Olver–Townsend operators, generalized tau, per-group plans.

DiscretizationError([message, where, why, ...])

The expression cannot be discretized as requested (explicit reason given).

conatus.errors¶

Public exception hierarchy of Conatus (v0.1).

ConatusError([message, where, why, fix, doc])

Base class.

EquationError([message, where, why, fix, doc])

An equation (or the whole problem) is inconsistent with the problem type or variables.

UndeclaredVariableError([message, where, ...])

A field is used as an unknown (or needs data) but is neither a variable nor given data.

ParameterError([message, where, why, fix, doc])

A parameter has no value, or a parameter name is unknown.

BoundaryConditionError([message, where, ...])

Boundary conditions are missing, in excess, or placed where none is possible.

EquationSyntaxError([message, text, position])

A string equation does not follow the declared grammar (conatus.ir.parse.GRAMMAR) or names something that is not in its declared namespace.

ResolutionError([message, where, why, fix, doc])

The resolution is too small for what is asked (boundary conditions, derivatives...).

CoordinateError([message, where, why, fix, doc])

Inconsistent coordinates, bases or domain.

OptionError([message, where, why, fix, doc])

An option has an invalid value (scheme, backend, method...); close names are suggested.

InputTypeError([message, where, why, fix, doc])

An argument has the wrong type (a Parameter, a Field, a number was expected...).

DtypeError([message, where, why, fix, doc])

Real/complex (dtype) mismatch; conversions are never implicit.

TensorError([message, where, why, fix, doc])

Tensor signatures do not match (scalar vs vector, different coordinate systems).

ConvergenceError([message, where, why, fix, doc])

An iteration did not reach its declared tolerance within its declared budget.

NumericalError([message, where, why, fix, doc])

A computed state is not finite (NaN or inf): a time integration blew up.

UnderResolvedWarning

A running simulation is under-resolved: the relative spectral tail of an unknown along a direction exceeds the declared RUN_TAIL_TOL (measured at the finite-state check; warned once per run and recorded by explain()).

IllPosedWarning

An implicit term makes the initial-value problem ill-posed: its leading-order growth rate increases without bound with the wavenumber (backward diffusion, a negative viscosity), detected when the model is compiled and named with the term.

InitialDataWarning

Callable initial data are not fully resolved: their relative spectral tail beyond the resolution (sampled on twice the grid) lies between INITIAL_TAIL_TOL and the refusal level INITIAL_TAIL_REFUSE (a coarse N of a resolution study); warned once per initial(...) call and recorded by explain().

BackendError([message, where, why, fix, doc])

The requested backend (GPU, compiled core) is not available here.

MissingDependencyError([message, where, ...])

An optional package or the compiled core is missing.

MissingFileError([message, where, why, fix, doc])

A file to read (an output, a checkpoint) does not exist, or is a directory.

UnsupportedError([message, where, why, fix, doc])

Declared in the IR but not implemented (yet) for this case.

AmbiguousNameError([message, where, why, ...])

A name means two different things (two modules export it); use one of the explicit names.

ResolutionWarning

A returned result is under-resolved by a declared measure: the mode an eigenvalue result selects has its energy in the last coefficients, a Floquet-Hill mode in its outermost harmonics.

CorrectnessWarning

The returned answer may not be the one the call meant: it is about another base state, another group of modes, or a choice the call did not declare.

BaseStateWarning

A linearization about a base state that is not a steady solution: its steady residual exceeds BASE_RESIDUAL_TOL and base_residual='accept' was not given, so the modes are not the normal modes of a flow that exists (accepted in v0.1, refused in v0.2).

GroupWarning

An eigenvalue call without a group (no k= on a periodic model, no m=/kz=/ ell= on a curvilinear one) solved one group only (k = 0, or the first group): the modes of every other wavenumber are absent from the result (required in v0.2).

DefaultChoiceWarning

A choice that decides the answer was left out and the v0.1 default or guess was used: the growth rule of an eigenvalue guessed from its name (m.eigenvalue("c") ranks by Im c), the top condition of this case (no-slip).

ExperimentalWarning

A feature of the experimental list was used (once per feature and process): it is shipped and tested, but not promised: its API and defaults may change in a minor release without a deprecation cycle, and some of its claims rest on fewer measurements.
