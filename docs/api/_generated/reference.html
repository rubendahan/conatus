Every public object re-exported by conatus.expert, grouped by the module that defines it and documented from its docstring.

Verification tools (spec §68–86): MMS, convergence studies, reports — never a CORRECT flag.

Check(name, value, threshold, what)

VerificationReport(checks)

verify(pevp, mode[, critical, rel_h])

Residuals, rank, reconstruction, spectral tail, sensitivity Taylor test, critical point.

conatus.ir¶

Mathematical IR: coordinates, bases, domains, fields, expressions, problems.

Coordinate(name, system, index)

One coordinate of a coordinate system (e.g. x of Cartesian (x, y)).

CoordinateSystem(*names)

Base class: an ordered tuple of coordinates with a metric.

CartesianCoordinates(*names)

Cartesian coordinates in 1, 2 or 3 dimensions (identity metric).

PolarCoordinates(azimuth, radius)

(phi, r) on the disk or annulus.

CylindricalCoordinates(azimuth, radius, axial)

(phi, r, z) for pipes and periodic cylinders.

S2Coordinates(azimuth, colatitude)

(phi, theta) on the sphere of radius R.

SphericalCoordinates(azimuth, colatitude, radius)

(phi, theta, r) for the ball and the spherical shell.

Basis(coord, size, bounds[, dealias, grid])

Base class for 1D spectral bases attached to one coordinate.

RealFourier(coord, size, bounds[, dealias, grid])

Real Fourier series (cos/sin pairs) for real-valued data.

ComplexFourier(coord, size, bounds[, ...])

Complex Fourier series exp(i k x).

Jacobi(coord, size, bounds, a, b[, dealias, ...])

Jacobi polynomials P_n^(a,b) on a bounded interval (mapped from [-1, 1]).

Chebyshev(coord, size, bounds[, dealias, grid])

Chebyshev T_n = Jacobi(-1/2, -1/2) up to normalisation.

ChebyshevT

ChebyshevU(coord, size, bounds[, dealias])
